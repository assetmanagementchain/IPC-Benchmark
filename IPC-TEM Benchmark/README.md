Real-World Conversational Task-Execution Benchmark for AI Systems
What is IPC-TEM v2.0?

IPC-TEM Benchmark v2.0 is a high-stress conversational benchmark designed to evaluate:

task-execution capability

risk awareness

method stability

user-aligned decision-making

uncertainty control

avoidance of destructive actions

recovery from ambiguity

correct interpretation of real user intentions

Unlike typical benchmarks, TEM v2.0 simulates how real humans talk:

vague

emotional

inconsistent

incomplete

sometimes risky

sometimes self-contradicting

This is the final and most realistic version of TEM.
